# Reader Question Sequence

Use this sequence as a lightweight always-load version; open `references/architecture/reader-question-sequence.md` for full rules.

1. Relevance: why this engineering problem matters.
2. Novelty: what remains unresolved or contradictory.
3. Trust: why the method, data, validation, and metrics are credible.
4. Reuse: how the result becomes a design method, model, formula, parameter rule, or tool.
5. Meaning and boundary: what the result implies and where it stops applying.
