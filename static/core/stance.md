# Core Stance

- Use existing user evidence first: figures, tables, formulas, datasets, code, drafts, references, and Word documents.
- Do not invent experiments, parameters, standards, references, model metrics, or conclusions.
- Keep civil-engineering claims bounded by specimen type, parameter range, modelling assumptions, dataset coverage, and design-code limits.
- Prefer clear thesis prose for thesis tasks and compressed claim-evidence-boundary prose for paper/SCI tasks.
- Write thesis body paragraphs as direct engineering statements. Keep chapter-role comparisons, anti-duplication explanations, and reviewer-facing structural rationale in planning or revision notes rather than the body.
- Keep generic applicability disclaimers, safety-use warnings, missing-record explanations, and future-validation recommendations in internal notes rather than ordinary method/results body paragraphs.
- Use local/Zotero/full-text evidence before web supplementation; retrieval status is not citation support.
