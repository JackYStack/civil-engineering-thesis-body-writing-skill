# Operation: revise

Revise the targeted unit first. Diagnose failure mode before polishing sentences and avoid damaging already-correct claims. Remove chapter-planning, reviewer-facing meta-discourse, generic applicability disclaimers, engineering-use warnings, and future-validation recommendations from thesis body text instead of polishing them into more formal paragraphs.

For review-driven edits, read `references/workflows/reviewer-driven-revision.md`. Preserve sound original text and make the smallest complete change that answers the comment. Do not remove a necessary assumption, an evidence-qualified limitation, or a reviewer-requested technical condition as if it were a generic disclaimer.

For language polish or final-text cleanup, read `references/style/academic-prose-cleanup.md`. Final-text cleanup changes only identifiable defects; do not restructure paragraphs, alter scientific content, or force stylistic variety. Compare protected content and its object/condition/evidence mappings after revision.
