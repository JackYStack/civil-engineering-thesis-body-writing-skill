# Mode: paper-sci

Convert thesis material into one article-level argument. Prioritize central question, contribution, decisive evidence, compact Results and Discussion, and reviewer risk.

Use `references/architecture/evidence-led-narrative-and-defensive-writing.md` to organize the article around its strongest supported contribution. Remove gratuitous self-critique, but preserve material trade-offs, contrary findings, and necessary conditions. Narrow claims instead of asserting unsupported superiority.

For an existing manuscript under review, use `references/workflows/reviewer-driven-revision.md` and preserve its accepted structure unless the comment requires broader change. A local amendment does not require rebuilding the article-level argument.
