# Mode: thesis-audit

Use deterministic or evidence-based thesis/project checks. Load `references/workflows/thesis-format-audit.md` for format/readiness tasks, identify the artifact source of truth, calibrate the applicable ruleset, report per-dimension status, and keep fixes bounded and preview-first. For the author-confirmed HFUT Word project, also load `references/style/hfut-thesis-word-objects.md` when checking figures, tables, captions, or equations.

For scientific/content audits instead load `references/workflows/research-integrity-and-reproducibility.md` and the object-integrity guide as relevant. For final or cross-section work use `references/workflows/manuscript-consistency-and-delivery.md`. Do not assume every audit is a format-compliance task.
